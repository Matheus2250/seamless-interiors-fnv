# Seamless Interiors — Fallout: New Vegas

Walk into a building with **no loading screen**. For real: the interior is placed inside the
actual building in the open world. The door becomes a regular door that swings open, and you
just walk in.

> **Status: experimental.** The Goodsprings General Store works. Everything else is under
> construction and most of it has never been opened in game. See [Progress](#progress) and
> [Known issues](#known-issues).

## How it works

In the base game, a building's door is a teleport to another cell. Here the pipeline:

1. copies every object of the interior into the building in the open world, aligned on the door;
2. cuts the doorway out of the building's facade and out of the interior wall (visuals and collision);
3. replaces the load door with a regular animated door;
4. brings along the NPCs, their AI packages and the interior navmesh.

Everything is generated by Python scripts (standard library only), **with no GECK, FNVEdit or
NifSkope**. The repository contains no game files: the plugin and the models are generated from
your own installation.

## Progress

The game has **283** interiors with a load door to the open world.

| State | Interiors |
|---|---:|
| Converted and passing the automated checks | 72 |
| Converted but rejected (only fit when shrunk too much) | 56 |
| Cannot be converted today | 155 |

Of the 72 that pass, what has actually been seen **in game**:

| Interior | Result |
|---|---|
| Goodsprings General Store | Works: walk in, Chet behind the counter, trading, sleep routine |
| Prospector Saloon (Goodsprings) | You can walk in; it has visual defects (below) |
| Victor's Shack | Interior loads; door not evaluated yet |
| The other 69 | Never tested in game |

Why 155 are left out (approximate numbers):

- **~65** have terrain above their floor: basements, caves and sewers would end up buried.
- **~50** have no single building at the door: the structure is assembled from many pieces
  (casinos, the NCR prison, the Nellis hangars, Big MT).
- **~21** have more than one exterior door, in positions that do not agree with each other.
- **~19** have an interior much larger than the building.

## Known issues

**Requirement**
- **Needs the 4 GB executable** (FNV 4GB Patcher, or the GOG version). Without it the Steam game
  runs out of its 2 GB and crashes in pathfinding (`FalloutNV.exe+0x6A55A6`).

**General**
- The HUD shows "Mojave Wasteland" inside converted interiors.
- Interior lighting comes from the exterior.
- Converted doors are no longer locked at night.
- Objects rotated on more than one axis (books, small items) may sit slightly crooked.
- Quests and dialogue that test "is in cell X" are not rewritten (2 known interiors).
- Conversions move persistent references: use a new game.

**Prospector Saloon**
- With the door open, seen from outside, the doorway shows as a flat yellow sheet. Cause unknown.
- Interior pieces poke through the facade near a window.
- There was one crash inside the saloon (`+0x704CC`, tied to an actor) that did not happen again
  and was not investigated.

**Shrunk interiors**
- To fit the building, some interiors are scaled down (75% at most). Ceilings and passages get
  lower.

## Requirements

- Fallout: New Vegas 1.4.0.525 (tested on the Steam version, with the DLCs).
- The 4 GB executable.
- Python 3 (tested with 3.14), standard library only. Windows.

## Building

The game path is set in `tools/si/master.py` (default: the Steam installation).

```bash
cd tools
py -I si/inventory.py --refresh     # inventory of FalloutNV.esm (once)
py -I pipeline.py                   # Goodsprings General Store only -> out/build/Data
py -I pipeline.py --all --out out/exp/all     # all 72 configs (experimental)
py -I verify_build.py               # geometric checks
py -I verify_navmesh.py out/build/Data/SeamlessInteriors.esp
```

To install, copy `SeamlessInteriors.esp` and the `meshes/SeamlessInteriors` folder from the build
into the game's `Data` folder and enable the plugin.
